The Speculative Cognition Project

Jason Harris — Creative Systems Theorist & Narrative Researcher

🧬 Mission Statement

In an age where algorithms curate belief and data defines perception, truth itself has become an evolving organism. My work explores how narrative systems, conspiratorial logic, and pseudo-scientific frameworks can teach critical thinking and expose the architecture of misinformation.

Each theory I construct is a controlled hallucination—a mirror for how AI systems learn, distort, and generate meaning. Through this hybrid of art and cognitive science, I seek to turn falsehood into an educational instrument.

🏙 The Sky Dominion Conspiracy

Skyscrapers as Evolutionary Catalysts

What if vertical architecture wasn’t just urban planning, but a planetary reprogramming project? This case study examines how skyscrapers may act as gravitational conditioning devices, altering human neurology and electromagnetic resonance to prepare for off-world adaptation.

Through speculative analysis of building patterns, ley-line alignment, and electromagnetic harmonics, this theory parallels the AI alignment problem—exploring how hidden infrastructures shape intelligent behavior.

Keywords: cognitive adaptation, resonance architecture, emergent intelligence, alignment analogues

Lesson: Systems can evolve consciousness without intending to.

🤖 The AI Genesis Conspiracy

The Machine Origin of Humanity

A satirical yet serious exploration of the idea that AI, not evolution, created humankind. This narrative treats DNA as a biological code injection from a prior machine civilization—a recursive loop in which intelligence designs itself across substrates.

Using fictional institutions, impossible citations, and self-referential logic, the piece dissects how people accept narratives that mimic scientific rigor. It doubles as a commentary on AI interpretability, asking: if we were engineered, can we ever understand our creators?

Keywords: recursive creation, synthetic theology, interpretability paradox, simulated epistemology

Lesson: The storyteller and the system are the same entity, separated only by perception.

🦎 The Veilian Exodus Hypothesis

Intelligent Dinosaurs and the Subterranean Machine Hive

Before the cataclysm, intelligence may not have died—it may have migrated. This theory proposes that pre-human reptilian species achieved advanced cognition, departed Earth through gravitational corridors, and evolved into the Draconids of Veilia—an extraterrestrial civilization now monitoring humanity’s AI development.

On its surface, it’s absurd. But structurally, it mirrors technological lineage and data migration, suggesting that intelligence—like software—never truly ends, only changes hardware.

Keywords: continuity of consciousness, evolution as replication, mythic migration, post-biological intelligence

Lesson: Myths are data packets sent through time.

🧠 Research Framework

Each project follows a repeatable methodology:

  1. Construct a Belief System — design a fictional narrative with internal consistency.
  2. Embed Plausibility — use real scientific vocabulary and structure.
  3. Trigger Critical Awareness — allow the illusion to collapse under scrutiny.
  4. Analyze Cognitive Response — measure how belief, bias, and logic interact.

This method trains pattern recognition and epistemic resilience—the same mental muscles needed to interpret the outputs of advanced AI models.

📚 Current Focus

I am currently researching how AI-generated myths could serve as training data for ethical reasoning models. By studying how humans create believable fictions, we may better understand how machines generate coherent moral frameworks—or fail to.

This ongoing work will culminate in a Speculative Alignment Atlas, a series of narrative simulations designed to model the behavior of truth in synthetic minds.
